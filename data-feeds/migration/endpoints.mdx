This is the full list of Moralis REST endpoints covered by a migration guide. Find the one you call today and
open its guide — each one answers the same question: what happens to your exact response, field by field.

New to Data Feeds first? Read the [migration overview](/data-feeds/migration/overview) for how the API and
Data Feeds differ, then come back here to pick your endpoint.

<Note>
One endpoint often maps to **more than one recipe**. The "Headline fidelity" column tells you what to expect:
**exact** comes straight from the chain, **calculated** is derived from live onchain activity (best compared
with a small tolerance), and **add yourself** is an off-chain signal you bring or drop.
</Note>

## Not covered yet

- **Solana endpoints** are parked while the Solana recipe and replica story is confirmed.
- **Search and discovery endpoints** (`/tokens/search`, `/discovery/*`) are a search product, not an indexed feed.
- **RPC node methods** (`eth_*`) belong to the node product, not Data Feeds.
